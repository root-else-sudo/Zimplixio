## What Is Zimplixio?

Zimplixio is an SMB technology consulting firm that brings enterprise-grade expertise to growing businesses — without the enterprise overhead. The company's founding premise is that most growing businesses are stuck in a gap: they've outgrown off-the-shelf tools, but they aren't yet ready (or don't want to pay for) a full internal engineering team or a large consulting firm.

**Core positioning statement:** *"Enterprise expertise simplified for SMBs."*

**Target market:** Small-to-medium businesses — typically 10 to 500 employees — where technology is starting to slow them down instead of move them forward.

---

## The Problem Zimplixio Solves

Growing businesses often face a technology clarity problem, not just a technology problem. They have tools that don't talk to each other, processes that are still manual, and no clear roadmap for what to fix first or how to build what comes next. Large consulting firms are expensive and impersonal; generic software products don't fit their specific needs; and hiring an internal engineering team is premature or too costly.

Zimplixio's founders observed this gap firsthand while working at enterprise companies (PayPal, Hewlett Packard Enterprise, IBM, Intel) — they saw what enterprise-level technology infrastructure and process discipline could do, and they built a firm to bring that same caliber of expertise to SMBs at an accessible scale.

## How Zimplixio Works — The Engagement Process

Zimplixio uses a PDCA (Plan → Do → Check → Act) continuous improvement methodology. Every engagement follows a four-phase process:

### Phase 1: Discovery Call (Week 1)
A free 30–60-minute conversation. No pitch — just an honest look at the client's challenges, their current stack, and whether Zimplixio is the right fit. There is no obligation and no sales pressure.

### Phase 2: Assessment (Weeks 1–2)
Zimplixio maps the client's current state, identifies the gaps that matter most, and defines a clear scope — so the client knows exactly what they're getting before any work begins. No surprises.

### Phase 3: Execution (Ongoing)
Work is done in focused sprints with regular check-ins. Clients get access to a shared project board so progress is visible in real time, not just at the end of a project. Zimplixio does not disappear after kickoff.

### Phase 4: Deliver & Evolve (Delivery & Beyond)
Full documentation and knowledge transfer at delivery. For clients who want to keep building, Zimplixio stays embedded as long as needed (transitioning into the Embedded Technical Partnership service).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How is Zimplixio different from a typical consulting firm?**
Most consulting firms send a team the client has never met, built around whoever is available. At Zimplixio, every engagement is led directly by the founding team — senior engineers and architects with 15+ years of experience. They don't advise from the sidelines; they build, implement, and stay until it works.

**What size companies do you work with?**
Small-to-medium businesses — typically 10 to 500 employees — that have outgrown ad-hoc solutions but aren't ready for a full internal engineering team.

**How long does a typical engagement last?**
A strategy assessment typically takes 1–2 weeks. A full implementation project runs 2–6 months. Work is done in clearly defined phases — each one produces a tangible deliverable and requires explicit approval before the next begins. Clients are never locked into more than they've agreed to.

**Do you work with specific technologies?**
Zimplixio is technology-agnostic. The job is to recommend what's right for the client's situation, not to push preferred tools. The team has hands-on expertise across backend and frontend frameworks, relational and cloud databases, major cloud platforms, data engineering tools, and agentic AI. They start every engagement by understanding what the client already has and build on it wherever it makes sense.

**Do you work with companies outside the US?**
Yes. Zimplixio has worked with clients across North America, Latin America, and Europe. The team operates remotely by default, so geography is not a barrier.
