Understanding the Metric

What is NPS?

The Net Promoter Score (NPS) is a widely-used measure of customer loyalty and satisfaction. Clients answer one simple question: "How likely are you to recommend us to a colleague?" on a scale of 0-10.

Scores range from -100 to +100. Any score above 0 is good, above 50 is excellent, and above 70 is world-class. For context, top consulting firms typically score between 40 and 60.

-100 0 +100
Needs improvement Good Excellent World-class

How We Measure

Our Methodology

We survey every client at the conclusion of each engagement. Surveys are anonymous and administered via a neutral third-party tool. We publish our aggregate score transparently because we believe in accountability and earning trust through results.
